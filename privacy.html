Matejcek Listing Aid Privacy Policy

```

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Matejcek Listing Aid is a private application used to assist with creating and managing eBay listings. The application is operated for a small number of users and is not offered as a public commercial service.

Information We Access

When an eBay user authorizes Matejcek Listing Aid, the application may access information provided by eBay that is necessary to create and manage listings on that user's behalf. This may include eBay account information, listing information, inventory information, and other information required by the eBay APIs used by the application.

The application may also process listing information and photographs supplied by the user for the purpose of preparing and publishing eBay listings.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay is used only to provide the functionality requested by the authorized user, including creating, publishing, and managing eBay listings.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise provide personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Authentication and Security

Matejcek Listing Aid uses eBay's OAuth authorization process. Users authorize the application through eBay and may revoke that authorization through their eBay account.

The application does not request, collect, or store an eBay user's eBay password. Application credentials and OAuth credentials are treated as confidential and are not intentionally disclosed to other parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to perform the functions of the application. Personal information will be deleted when it is no longer necessary for those purposes, when the user requests its deletion, or when continued retention is no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Third-Party Services

Matejcek Listing Aid uses eBay APIs to interact with eBay services. Information transmitted to eBay through the application is handled in accordance with eBay's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of Matejcek Listing Aid or its data practices change. The current version will be made available at this URL.
```
